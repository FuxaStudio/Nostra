1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is Capella Nostra, [TO BE COMPLETED: legal form, registered office and company ID]. You can contact us about data protection matters at capellanostra@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Through the contact form on this website we process only the data you provide to us yourself:

  • your first name and surname,
  • your e-mail address,
  • your phone number (if you provide one),
  • the content of your message.

3. Purpose and legal basis of processing

We process your data solely in order to handle your enquiry or question and for the related communication. The legal basis for the processing is taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract, together with our legitimate interest in answering your question (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. Retention period

We keep the data from the enquiry form for as long as is necessary to handle the enquiry and any follow-up communication, and for no longer than [TO BE COMPLETED: e.g. 2 years] from the last contact. After that we delete it.

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data or share it for marketing purposes. It is handled only by providers of the technical services that the website and our e-mail communication depend on:

  • Web3Forms – delivers messages from the form to our mailbox,
  • Google (Gmail) – we keep the messages in the ensemble’s mailbox,
  • Cloudflare – the website runs on its servers.

These providers may process data outside the European Union as well, in particular in the USA. Any such transfer takes place on the basis of safeguards under the GDPR, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses no analytical or marketing cookies and does not track your behaviour. Only the technical data necessary for the site to function is stored.

The font, photographs and all other files of the site are loaded directly from this website, not from third-party services. Links to Google Maps, Google Calendar, Facebook or Instagram lead to the websites of those services, which are governed by their own policies.

7. Your rights

In connection with the processing of personal data you have the right:

  • to access your personal data,
  • to have inaccurate data corrected,
  • to have your data erased (the “right to be forgotten”),
  • to restrict the processing,
  • to object to the processing,
  • to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

To exercise your rights, contact us at capellanostra@gmail.com.
