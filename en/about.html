About us

The Capella Nostra Chamber Ensemble

Classical music live — properly rehearsed, and played where it sounds best.

Who we are

We play the classics for the people in the hall, not for the display case

We are Capella Nostra — a chamber ensemble born out of the urge to play classical music to the full. We keep to the score, but we play it alive and with feeling. We believe that classical music played well speaks to anyone who takes a seat in the hall.

Our story

It started with evenings in a rehearsal room

At the start there were only a handful of us. We met in the evenings in a single rehearsal room, played mostly for ourselves, and in time realised it would be a shame to keep it to ourselves.

The ensemble has been led from the beginning by its founder and conductor, Jan Kohout. Those chamber rehearsals under his baton grew into a first concert series and, a few years later, an album of our own. Today we play dozens of concerts a year.
